2. Desktop Engineering Guide: How to Build a 3x RTX 3060 PC

Installing three consumer graphics cards into a single desktop PC requires careful component selection to prevent physical conflicts, PCIe lane choking, and thermal throttling.

A. Motherboard Selection & PCIe Slot Spacing

Consumer RTX 3060 cards are 2-slot or 2.5-slot thick with open-air axial fans. If placed in adjacent PCIe slots, Card 1 and Card 2 will be completely choked for intake air and thermal throttle to 90°C.

  • Recommended Motherboard Type: ATX or E-ATX with 3 physical PCIe x16 slots spaced at Slot 1, Slot 4, and Slot 7 (a 3-slot pitch between cards).
    • Examples: ASUS ProArt Z690/Z790 Creator WiFi, ASRock Z690/Z790 Taichi, or MSI MEG Z690 Unify.
  • PCIe Lane Bifurcation: Modern consumer CPUs provide 16 lanes from the CPU (running at x8/x8 or x8/x4/x4), with the third slot routed via the motherboard chipset (PCH) at PCIe 4.0 x4.
    • In llama.cpp layer pipelining, only small hidden state tensors (~4KB to 16KB per token) are exchanged across PCIe. Even PCIe 4.0 x4 provides 8 GB/s bandwidth, meaning the chipset-routed card suffers <2% decode latency!
  • Alternative Mounting (Riser Cables): Use a standard motherboard and mount GPU #2 and GPU #3 vertically or in an open-frame / bottom bracket using high-speed PCIe 4.0 x16 shielded riser cables (e.g. LINKUP or Cooler Master).

B. Power Supply & Cable Requirements

Powering three modern GPUs requires dedicated 12V rails and adequate physical cabling:

  • GPU Power Draw: 3x 170W TDP = 510W GPU power under full compute load.
  • Total System Power: Combined with an Intel Core i5/i7 or AMD Ryzen 7 (120–160W) and peripherals (~60W), total load reaches ~720W.
  • Recommended PSU: 850W or 1000W 80+ Gold / Platinum PSU (e.g., Corsair RM1000x, Seasonic Focus GX-850/1000, or be quiet! Pure Power 12 M 1000W).
  • Dedicated Power Leads: Ensure the PSU includes 3 separate, independent 8-pin PCIe cables. Do NOT daisy-chain all three cards from a single split cable.

C. PC Case & Airflow Architecture

  • Case Selection: Requires a chassis with at least 8 expansion slots and bottom intake fan mounts.
    • Top Picks: Lian Li O11 Dynamic EVO XL, Fractal Torrent, or Corsair 5000D / 7000D Airflow.
  • Airflow Dynamics: Install 3x 120mm or 2x 140mm high-static pressure fans at the floor of the case blowing cool air directly upward into the intake fans of all three graphics cards.
Software & OS Advantage of 3x 3060:
Unlike server cards like the Tesla P40, the RTX 3060 is a consumer card with native HDMI 2.1 and DisplayPort 1.4a outputs on every card. It is 100% plug-and-play in both Windows 11 and Linux, requires zero custom cooling shrouds or EPS adapter cables, and is automatically recognized by standard NVIDIA Game Ready and Studio drivers.
